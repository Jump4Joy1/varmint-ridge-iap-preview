Privacy

Varmint Ridge is a family game from JW Manor. It is for all ages. It has no ads and no tracking.

Scores, the theme you pick, sound, and a saved game stay on this device only (in local storage). Nothing is sent to a server. There is no account.

The Supporter Store is off in the browser. A future App Store build can sell a coin pouch, the Ridge Pass, and a starter bundle through Apple. Every purchase and Restore Purchases sits behind a grown-up math question. Those sales are handled by Apple, not by this page.

Home-screen widgets read the best score from the app on this phone. They do not use your name, location, or contacts.
